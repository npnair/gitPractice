## What's going on here

- **GitHub** is a website where programmers store their code and projects online.
- A **repository** (or "repo") is like a folder for one project. This one is called `gitPractice`.
- **Pushing** means sending changes from a computer up to GitHub, so they're saved online and anyone can see them.
- Every change is saved as a **commit**, which records what changed, who changed it, and when. Click "History" or "Commits" to see the full timeline.

## Why it matters

Employers look at GitHub to see real projects, not just a resume. My NBA, NFL, and data projects all live here too.
